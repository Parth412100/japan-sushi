Our Story

Crafting Tradition
Since 1987

Nestled in the historic Gion district of Kyoto, Umi Sakura has been perfecting the art of Edomae-style sushi for over three generations. Our master chefs train for years to master the delicate balance of vinegared rice and the freshest seafood.

Every piece of sushi served tells a story — from the carefully aged tuna to the hand-pressed rice that holds its shape with just the right amount of give. We believe in honoring tradition while embracing the finest ingredients Japan has to offer.

Master Chef

Tanaka Hiroshi

第三代目 · 板前長

With 35 years of experience behind the counter, Chef Tanaka inherited the craft from his grandfather who founded Umi Sakura in 1987. Trained in the strict Edomae tradition, he spent a decade at a three-Michelin-starred establishment in Ginza before returning to lead the family business.

Chef Tanaka personally selects every piece of fish at Toyosu Market before dawn, ensuring only the finest quality reaches your plate. His philosophy: "Sushi is not made — it is born from the harmony between nature and the craftsman's spirit."

"The rice is the soul of sushi. Everything else is a guest."

Tanaka Hiroshi
